🀄 Chinese Mahjong Rules

🎯 Objective

Be the first to complete a winning hand of 14 tiles consisting of 4 sets (melds) and 1 pair.

🃏 Types of Melds

Sequence (Chow/吃) 🀇🀈🀉 3 consecutive tiles of the same suit. Only numbered suits. Only from the player to your left.
Triplet (Pong/碰) 🀙🀙🀙 3 identical tiles. Can claim from any player's discard.
Kong (Kan/槓) 🀄🀄🀄🀄 4 identical tiles. Draw an extra tile from the wall after declaring.
Pair (雀頭) 🀀🀀 2 identical tiles. Every winning hand needs exactly one pair.

🔄 Turn Flow

  1. Draw a tile from the wall
  2. If you can win (胡 Hu), declare it!
  3. Discard one tile from your hand
  4. Other players may claim your discard (Pon/Chi/Kan/Win)
  5. If claimed, the claiming player discards; if not, next player draws

🏆 Winning Hands

Standard Hand 4 melds (sets) + 1 pair = 14 tiles
Seven Pairs (七對子) 7 pairs of identical tiles — a special winning hand
Thirteen Orphans (十三么) One each of 1m,9m,1p,9p,1s,9s,E,S,W,N,中,發,白 + any duplicate = 14 tiles

⭐ Scoring (Fan)

Self-draw win (Tsumo)+1 Fan
Concealed hand+1 Fan
Dragon triplet+1 Fan each
Round/Seat wind triplet+1 Fan each
All one suit+3 Fan
Seven pairs+3 Fan
All triplets+2 Fan

🎮 Controls (Bot Game)

  • Draw: Draw a tile from the wall
  • Click tile: When 14 tiles in hand, click to discard
  • 碰 Pon: Claim discard for a triplet
  • 吃 Chi: Claim left player's discard for a sequence
  • 槓 Kan: Declare a kong (4 of a kind)
  • 胡 Win!: Declare a winning hand
  • Pass: Skip claiming a discard
  • Sort ↕: Sort tiles in hand by suit
